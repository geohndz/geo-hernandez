Where the timings land

Duration is a budget. A 700ms first-load hero can earn the time. A 700ms Reveal on the fourth about paragraph, and a 500ms fade on every sidebar click, cannot. Each dot is a motion that already ships.

Instant / Responsive · 0–300ms — tabs, hovers, nav pills, press feedback.
Deliberate · 300–500ms — drawers, first-load hero, large case-study surfaces.
Sluggish · 500ms+ — reserve for the intro and about stickers — not every route and paragraph.
What's off

The motions a visitor hits on every page — route fade (4) and Reveal (6) — sit in deliberate-to-sluggish. The case-study tabs they actually poke (1) have no content motion at all. Frequency and duration are inverted.

Tab chrome slides. Tab content teleports.

WhatThe case-study galleries animate the active pill, then remount the media with a key and swap the caption instantly. F1Modules replaces the image with no fade. TeacherTools remounts the video. F1Directions fades the pictures and snaps the pro/con lists.

Why it mattersA polished control that dumps new content in place reads as unfinished. The eye follows the pill, then the stage pops. That is the opposite of invisible enhancement.

Recommended motionKeep stacked panels mounted. Crossfade with opacity + translateY(6px) + blur(3px) over 220ms using cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Exit is subtler: opacity only, 160ms. Do not remount on key unless the video source must change — then fade the outgoing frame first.

Hover scales and 500–700ms lifts are the default card recipe

WhatPlay buttons on four showcases use ungated hover:scale-105. Project and study cards lift and zoom the image over 500–700ms. Chip accents transition color for 500ms. No (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) gate, so tap on iOS can stick a hover scale.

Why it mattersThe same micro-bounce on every card is the hover-scale-on-everything tell. Hover color at 500ms feels soggy. Press is missing entirely — the hand gets scale on hover and nothing on click.

Recommended motionDrop hover scale on play controls. Gate remaining hover to fine pointers. Card hover: border + background only, 180ms ease. Image zoom, if kept, 1.02 over 280ms. Add :active { transform: scale(0.97) } with transition: transform 160ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) on the Email CTA and play buttons.

CriticalEmilAccessibility

Motion.js never sees prefers-reduced-motion

Whatapp/globals.css nukes CSS animation and transition durations. Hero, the route template, mobile nav, sidebar pill, tab pills, phase carousel, research board, evolution accordion, and Logo all drive motion through the Motion library. Those writes ignore the CSS hammer. Only Reveal, Stagger, IntroOverlay, AboutPortrait, BrandLockup, and Constraints call useReducedMotion().

Why it mattersReduced motion is not optional. A visitor who asked the OS to keep the page still still gets a 700ms hero, a sliding drawer, and layout springs. Vestibular safety fails on the first paint.

Recommended motionOne helper: if useReducedMotion() is true, skip initial/animate transforms and keep a 200ms opacity fade where the state change would otherwise be invisible. Apply it in Hero, app/template.tsx, MobileNav, and every layoutId tab (instant pill under RM). Do not rely on the global CSS splat for JS motion.

ImportantEmil

Accordions and spatial diagrams animate layout

WhatEvolution timeline expands with height: "auto". F1 spatial panels use transition-all duration-500 while left/width/top are inline percentages. The hamburger uses transition-all to tween width and rotation. Phase dots use transition-all. Constraints tween margin.

Why it mattersLayout properties cost layout + paint + composite. transition-all also catches color and border on the same clock. Rapid tabbing of the spatial model will jank before it feels spatial.

Recommended motionAccordion: grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr or clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0) over 240ms cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Spatial panels: list opacity, border-color only; snap left/width or drive them with transform. Hamburger: transition: transform 200ms, keep bars full width. Phase dots: transition: width 180ms, background-color 180ms.
