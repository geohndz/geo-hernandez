## Design Evolution

<StudyGroup>
### Phase 1: A personal MVP

I hadn't worked on this tool before, so I learned the system while building a modernized authoring MVP on my own. Stakeholders saw a larger opportunity: the English team could take over authoring instead of relying on us for every build and revision.

<EngPhases phase="1" />
</StudyGroup>

<StudyGroup>
### Phase 2: Integrating with the legacy system

The student-facing experience had to stay exactly as it was. I couldn't replace the implementation wholesale — new authoring features had to land in the original files. Four weeks to integrate and deliver the first lesson.

<EngPhases phase="2" />
</StudyGroup>

<StudyGroup>
### Phase 3: Stabilizing the workflow

Recurring problems fell away. Lessons no longer needed reconstruction after every feedback round. Once that process stabilized, production pace changed dramatically.

<EngPhases phase="3" />
</StudyGroup>

## Authoring Experience

<StudyGroup>
Authoring stopped being a code-adjacent chore. Inline formatting, clearer block controls, and safer deletes meant the English team could revise content the way they already thought about it.
